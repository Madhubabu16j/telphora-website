Company

Carriers across distances.

Telphora comes from the Greek tele — “far, distant” — and phora — “bearer, carrier.” That which carries across distances.

We’re a technology company at the intersection of cybersecurity, cloud infrastructure, and intelligent automation — building AI agents and security products that carry our customers’ most critical workloads safely across the cloud.

We started Telphora on a simple observation: attackers automate, defenders drown in alerts. The only way to win is to automate defense too — with agents that never sleep, never miss, and never get tired.

Founded 2026. Early-stage, and just getting started.

What we believe

Three principles. Zero compromises.

Assume breach

We design as if attackers are already inside — so they never get far. Every product decision starts from the adversary’s perspective.

Secure by default

Every deployment ships locked down. Hardening isn’t an opt-in, a checklist, or a phase two — it’s the starting point.

Prove it

Continuous verification and audit trails. Trust, but verify — and keep the receipts.

Who we serve

For teams that can’t afford a breach.

From two-person startups to large enterprises — if your business runs on the cloud, it runs on trust. We protect both.

Startups

No security team? No problem. Telphora’s agents are the security team — monitoring, triaging, and responding from day one, while you focus on building.

Enterprises

Scale your existing team’s reach. Agents absorb the alert flood and handle routine response — your experts spend their judgment where it counts.
